## The Hook

Ancient maps and relic fragments have surfaced.

Rival warbands race to find and control a daemon's prison before it opens on its own — **or is opened deliberately.**

Regardless of who wins any single game, every battle pushes the same story toward the same climax:

**the release of Vhar'Ghul, the Ashen Sovereign.**

*Works with any number of warbands — bring whichever factions want in.*

---

## Who's In (Current Roster)

Any faction can have a reason to want the prison. This campaign currently runs with:

| Faction | Why they want in |
|---|---|
| **Slaves to Darkness** | Their prophets have seen it: Vhar'Ghul can grant true ascension to a worthy champion. |
| **Horns of Hashut** | His imprisoned essence could fuel daemon-forges capable of building infernal engines like nothing seen before. |
| **Skaven** | The prison is packed with warpstone-tainted energy. Naturally, they intend to steal it before anyone else can. |

*Everyone wants the same prize. Nobody wants the same thing to happen to it. Adding a warband? Just needs a motive.*

Starting Order of Battle is capped at **1,000pts**, including the Warlord.

---

## Pairings — Three Players

Rounds 1–3 are a round robin (everyone plays twice, sits out once); Rounds 4–5 are a seeded knockout.

| Round | Match | Sits out |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Army1 vs Army2 | Army3 |
| 2 | Army2 vs Army3 | Army1 |
| 3 | Army3 vs Army1 | Army2 |
| 4 | Seed 1 vs Seed 2 (best round-robin records) | Seed 3 |
| 5 | Round 4 winner vs Seed 3 | — |
| 6 (Finale) | revealed after Round 5 | — |

*Draw lots for the Army1–3 seats before Round 1. Three-way tie after Round 3? Seed by total VP scored across the round robin, or dice off — agree before Round 4.*

---

## Why the Battleplans Fit

Every official battleplan maps naturally onto the narrative:

- **The Prize of Gallet** → searching for clues
- **The Hidden Cache** → finding ancient records
- **The Ritual** → activating seals
- **Power in Numbers** → gathering sacrifices and followers
- **The Vice** → assaulting the final seal
- **Focal Points / Nexus Collapse** → opening the prison

---

## Finale Trigger

From the **end of Round 3 onward**, roll:

### D6 + 1 per ritual reward held by any player

**On 8+ → the prison opens** and the finale narrative rules kick in.

*The more rewards claimed across the table, the sooner the wastelands run out of time.*

# Round 1 — Strange Omens

**Battleplan:** The Prize of Gallet (*The Trap*)

Scouting parties investigating the omens are ambushed by daemonic manifestations near the old ruins. Nobody knows where the prison lies yet — only that it already knows they're looking.

---

## Round 1 — Reference

**Setup:** Invader deploys first in their territory. Ambusher places units in their own territory (12"+ from enemies) or on terrain (3"+ from enemies). **Ambusher chooses who goes first.**

**Victory:** Invader scores 1 VP/round per unit wholly in enemy territory. Ambusher scores 2 VP each time an invader unit is destroyed.

**Key rules:** 800pts · 5 rounds · ambusher sets faction terrain · invader cannot use reserves

**Bonus Renown:** Invader units surviving to round 3; ambusher units that destroyed enemies.

**Reward:** *Fragment of the Seal* · **At the finale:** one free command point in round 1.

# Round 2 — The Search Begins

**Battleplan:** The Hidden Cache (*Breakthrough*)

The factions discover ancient vaults containing records that describe the daemon's prison.

---

## Round 2 — Reference

**Setup:** Invader deploys first (12"+ from opponent's edge). Custodian deploys in their territory without restriction. **Invader chooses first turn.**

**Victory:** Based on invader units that escape — half or more = major win; none = major loss; between depends on whether the general survived.

**Key rules:** 1000pts · 5 rounds · custodian sets faction terrain · invader units 7" of custodian's edge and 3"+ from enemies may leave play each round-end

**Bonus Renown:** Custodian units near their edge; invader units that escape.

**Reward:** *Ancient Coordinates* · **At the finale:** may redeploy one unit after deployment.

# Round 3 — The Ritual Sites

**Battleplan:** The Ritual

*"This is where the story really begins."*

Each faction discovers one of three ritual circles that form part of the daemon's prison — one force tries to activate it, another tries to stop it.

---

## Round 3 — Reference

**Setup:** Disruptor deploys first (9"+ from opponent). Ritualist deploys in their territory (6"+ from opponent). **Disruptor chooses who takes the first turn.**

**Victory:** Ritualist scores D6 VP for controlling the ritual site, +D3 per arcane repository controlled, −D3 per disruptor-controlled objective.

**Key rules:** 1250pts · 5 rounds · one ritual site + two arcane repositories placed · ritualist sets faction terrain

**Bonus Renown:** Units within 6" of any objective at battle's end.

**Reward:** *Ashen Sigil* · **At the finale:** one hero gains +1 control score.

**⚠ Finale trigger check begins this round →**

# Round 4 — Gathering Power

**Battleplan:** Power in Numbers (*Sudden Assault*)

The ritual sites are active, and the daemon whispers to mortals across the realm. Entire tribes vanish in the night. Every faction sweeps into contested territory to claim sacrifices and power before a rival does.

---

## Round 4 — Reference

**Setup:** Invader and custodian each deploy in their territory, 9"+ from battlefield center. **Invader chooses first player.**

**Victory:** 1 VP per battlefield quarter where a player has more friendly than enemy models at round's end.

**Key rules:** 1500pts · 5 rounds · battlefield split into quarters · custodian sets faction terrain

**Bonus Renown:** Units wholly within uncontested quarters (no enemies present).

**Reward:** *Daemonic Favour* · **Choose before finale:** one free All-out Attack **or** All-out Defence.

# Round 5 — The Final Seal

**Battleplan:** The Vice (*The Watchtower*)

Only one seal remains intact. The factions converge on the obsidian mountain that contains Vhar'Ghul's prison. This battle decides who stands closest when it opens.

---

## Round 5 — Reference

**Setup:** Guardian splits their army into garrison (deploys near the central watchtower, no reserves) and reinforcements (start in reserve). Invader deploys in their territory (9"+ from enemies). **Invader chooses first turn.**

**Victory:** Depends on invader control of the watchtower, whether it's ransacked (2 consecutive turns of control), and whether the guardian holds/defends it.

**Key rules:** 1750pts · 5 rounds · reinforcements arrive via guardian's movement phase (7" from edge, 3"+ from enemies)

**Bonus Renown:** Units within 6" of the watchtower at battle's end.

**Reward:** *Master of the Ritual* · **During the finale:** may choose priority roll result once.

# Round 6 — The Ashen Sovereign
## FINALE

**Battleplan:** The Nexus Collapse (*Convergence of Fate*, multi-player, Triumph & Treachery)

Multiple armies march on a strange monolith at the battlefield's center — **the Ashen Vault.** Only one can be the victor.

---

## Finale — Reference

**Points:** determined by the outcome of Round 5 — see the scenario page once Round 5 is decided.

**Composition:** Each player splits their army into vanguard + reinforcements (at least 1 vanguard unit per reinforcement unit). Faction terrain prohibited. Requires a **44" × 60"** battlefield.

**Setup:** Roll-off winner places terrain, including the central Ashen Vault. Roll-off determines territories/north edge; vanguards alternate placing 9"+ from enemy territory. Reinforcements arrive after each player's 2nd movement phase (6" from edge, 9"+ from enemies).

**Duration:** 4 battle rounds.

**Victory:** Control the Vault = major victory. No control but closest model = minor victory. Tied closest = draw.

*No official battleplans handy? Use Focal Points or Power in Numbers as the framework and bolt on the narrative rules below.*

---

## When the Prison Opens

Each round-end from Round 3 on: **D6 + 1 per ritual reward held by any player.** On **8+**, the prison opens.

**Vhar'Ghul emerges — not as a model, but as a battlefield effect, for the rest of the game:**

- Every unit suffers **D3 mortal wounds** on a natural 1 when running or charging
- Wizards gain **+1 to cast**
- Priests gain **+1 to chant**
- Heroes count as **+2 control**

*The battlefield descends into chaos.*
