Campaign-specific parameters on top of the generic *How to Create a Hero* procedure (core rules). Use this page for anything about hero creation that's specific to the Prison of Vhar'Ghul campaign rather than to Path to Glory in general.

## Starting Points

Starting Order of Battle (step 3 of the core procedure) is capped at **1,000pts** for this campaign, including the Warlord.
