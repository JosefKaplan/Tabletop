## Narrative

The omens began small. Ash falling on windless days. Carrion birds circling ruins that hold no dead. A trader found face-down at a crossroads, clutching a map drawn in a script no living scholar reads — and in three separate camps, three warlords are told the same rumour: *the prison is real, and it can be found.*

Scouting parties sent to investigate the omens have started coming back wrong — or not coming back at all. Around the old ruins, things move against the wind, and rival scouts stalk the same ground with the same orders: learn everything, leave no witnesses.

Nobody knows where the prison lies yet. Only that whatever stirs inside it already knows they are looking.

Full scouting forces (1000pts) are committed even this early — nobody wants to be caught under-strength if the rumour turns out to be true.

Wahapedia (4e battleplan — The Trap): https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/the-rules/narrative-play-games/#The-Trap

**Setup & Deployment:** One player is designated the invader, the other the ambusher. The invader deploys first within their territory. The ambusher places units either in their territory (12"+ from enemies) or on terrain features (3"+ from enemies).

**Victory Conditions:** Each round, the invader scores 1 victory point for each of their units wholly within enemy territory; the ambusher scores 2 victory points each time an invader unit is destroyed.

**Army Points:** 1000pts

**Key Rules:**
- Ambusher chooses who goes first
- 5 battle rounds total
- Ambusher can set faction terrain
- Invader cannot use reserves

**Bonus Renown:** Invader units surviving to round 3 gain renown; ambusher units that destroyed enemies gain renown.
