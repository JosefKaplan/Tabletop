## Narrative

The fragment's whispering leads down — to a vault-city sunk beneath the wastes, the buried archive of the civilisation that built the prison and then buried themselves alongside it, just to be sure.

Its shelves hold the only records of what sleeps in the mountain and how it was caged. Word has spread, and each side commits a full warband (1000pts) to the hunt: one force breaks into the vaults and must carry the records out through the ash, while its rivals throw a cordon across every road home.

What the raiders cannot carry, they burn. What they cannot burn, they memorise — badly.

## Battleplan
![Battleplan](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczN814XjQIXHvetoWynpvOomXt8U1O1wZFIfUd7U6AF0vfKkXwD03Gd3akvBWbzYtI8h-9-x1bgZ0nfY9iipTF1sr0DtlwXUZgqRf6encjcTo_bkZsY=w2400)


Wahapedia (4e battleplan — Breakthrough): https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/the-rules/narrative-play-games/#Breakthrough

**Setup & Deployment:** Invader deploys first (12"+ from opponent's edge). Custodian deploys within their territory without restrictions.

**Victory Conditions:** Based on how many invader units escape: half or more = major victory; none = major loss; some but less than half depends on whether general survived (minor victory/loss).

**Army Points:** 1000pts

**Key Rules:**
- At the end of each battle round, any invader units wholly within 7" of custodian's edge and 3"+ from enemies can leave play
- 5 rounds total
- Custodian sets faction terrain
- Invader chooses first turn

**Bonus Renown:** Custodian units near the custodian's edge gain renown; invader units that escape gain renown.
