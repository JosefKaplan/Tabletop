## Narrative

The ritual sites are active, and the daemon whispers to mortals across the realm. Entire tribes disappear in the night, drawn off to feed the ritual.

What is left in the contested territories — stragglers to press into service, relics to loot, veins of warp-touched power to bleed dry — will decide who stands strongest when the mountain finally opens. Every faction sweeps in at once, racing to strip the wastelands before a rival does.

Armies swell to full strength (1500pts) as each side commits everything it can spare.

## Battleplan
![Battleplan](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczPvbenljzK464S7txsMbhF2P8GPktFZMI0VPh87cIgsqS_i-QyMMpzAh0zewSEhNsLN0qB_Lb2KY225u33DQNCcaaowpKmscqOBozgxBTm6dcOjIKc=w2400)

Wahapedia (4e battleplan — Sudden Assault): https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/the-rules/narrative-play-games/#Sudden-Assault

**Setup & Deployment:** Invader and custodian each deploy within their territories, 9"+ from battlefield center. Invader chooses first player.

**Victory Conditions:** Each player scores 1 victory point for each quarter of the battlefield that has more friendly models wholly within it than enemy models at round's end.

**Army Points:** 1500pts

**Key Rules:**
- Battlefield divided into quarters
- 5 battle rounds
- Custodian sets faction terrain
- Victory determined by quarter control majority

**Bonus Renown:** Units wholly within uncontested quarters (no enemy units present) gain renown.
