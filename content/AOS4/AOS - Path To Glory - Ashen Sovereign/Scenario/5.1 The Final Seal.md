## Narrative

Only one seal remains intact — bound into a watchtower of black stone that the prison's builders raised on the mountain's slopes, so that something would always be watching.

The factions converge upon the obsidian mountain that contains Vhar'Ghul's prison, each fielding their largest host yet (1750pts). One army reaches the tower first and digs in behind its dead builders' walls. The others come up the slope to tear them off it.

This battle decides who will stand closest to the prison when it opens.

None of them plan to be standing anywhere else.

## Battleplan
![Battleplan](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMTPW0A9fFYNiDHvmi1aviYc2Qq-ytJTRD-RzuDobpLRmXv3g1d2F0ISm359hE4rrKXE-8tgtXR8dNF8vRhxdK4L9x7yUwLAnkoDlmjf-ZIu-oiIXg=w2400)

Wahapedia (4e battleplan — The Watchtower): https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/the-rules/narrative-play-games/#The-Watchtower

**Setup & Deployment:** Guardian splits army into garrison (deploys near center watchtower, no reserves) and reinforcements (starts in reserve). Invader deploys in their territory (9"+ from enemies).

**Victory Conditions:** Victory depends on: invader control of watchtower, whether it was ransacked (requires 2 consecutive turns of control), and whether guardian holds/defended it.

**Army Points:** 1750pts

**Key Rules:**
- If the invader controls the watchtower at the end of each of two consecutive turns, the watchtower is ransacked
- Reinforcements arrive via guardian's movement phase (7" from edge, 3"+ from enemies)
- 5 rounds total
- Invader chooses first turn

**Bonus Renown:** Units within 6" of watchtower at battle's end gain renown.


## Campaign Reward

### Winner gains

**Marked by Vhar'Ghul** 
One Hero of the winner's choice in their roster immediately gains a **free rank-up**.
 - Pick that Hero's next tier's 1-of-2 Path ability now, regardless of their current Renown total (their Renown number itself doesn't change, just their unlocked abilities).
 - If that Hero is already Legendary, this has no effect — there's no fifth tier to unlock.

### During the finale

May choose priority roll result once.
