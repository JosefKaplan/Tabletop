*HERO only · anyone can take it.* Turns a Hero into a personal combat monster — weapon and armour upgrades culminating in Ward or Strike-First. See [[Hero Paths]] for the shared rank structure and [[How to Create a Hero]] for the wider campaign rules.

## Rank Tree

| Rank | Renown | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aspiring | 5–14 | Berserker | Well of Strength |
| Elite | 15–29 | Martial Expertise | Powerful Presence |
| Mighty | 30–44 | Master-Crafted Armour | Master-Crafted Weapons |
| Legendary | 45+ | Warrior Without Equal | Unparalleled Duellist |

## Aspiring (5–14 Renown)

**Berserker** — You can re-roll charge rolls for this HERO.

**Well of Strength** — Heal (D6) this HERO once per battle.

## Elite (15–29 Renown)

**Martial Expertise** — Add 1 to hit rolls for this HERO's combat attacks.

**Powerful Presence** — Add D3 to this HERO's control score when contesting an uncontrolled objective.

## Mighty (30–44 Renown)

**Master-Crafted Armour** — Add 1 to save rolls for this HERO.

**Master-Crafted Weapons** — Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of this HERO's melee weapons.

## Legendary (45+ Renown)

**Warrior Without Equal** — This HERO has WARD (4+).

**Unparalleled Duellist** — This HERO has STRIKE-FIRST.
