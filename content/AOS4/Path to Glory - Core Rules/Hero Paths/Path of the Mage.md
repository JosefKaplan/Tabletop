*WIZARD HERO only.* Grants extra arcane tricks on top of the Hero's normal spell lore, culminating in battlefield-shaping spell-like abilities. See [[Hero Paths]] for the shared rank structure and [[How to Create a Hero]] for the wider campaign rules — remember to log each granted spell by name on the army's Spells Known column.

## Rank Tree

| Rank | Renown | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aspiring | 5–14 | Dedicated Practitioner | Eldritch Ward |
| Elite | 15–29 | Mystic Shield (casting 6) | Arcane Conduit (casting 3) |
| Mighty | 30–44 | Magical Might | Arcane Familiar |
| Legendary | 45+ | Eldritch Cataclysm (casting 8) | Sunder the Earth (casting 8) |

## Aspiring (5–14 Renown)

**Dedicated Practitioner** — Add 1 to casting rolls for this WIZARD.

**Eldritch Ward** — If this WIZARD makes a casting roll of 10+, they have WARD (5+) until your next turn.

## Elite (15–29 Renown)

**Mystic Shield** *(casting value 6)* — A friendly unit within 12" gains WARD (6+) until your next turn.

**Arcane Conduit** *(casting value 3)* — Add 1 to casting rolls for friendly WIZARDS until the end of the phase.

## Mighty (30–44 Renown)

**Magical Might** — Heal (D3) this WIZARD after they successfully cast a spell.

**Arcane Familiar** — Once per phase, you can re-roll 1 die when making a casting roll for this WIZARD.

## Legendary (45+ Renown)

**Eldritch Cataclysm** *(casting value 8)* — Pick an enemy unit within 15". Roll a dice for each model in that unit; for each 4+, it suffers 1 mortal damage.

**Sunder the Earth** *(casting value 8)* — Draw a line from a point within 18" of this WIZARD to the closest point on their base. Each unit crossed by that line suffers D3 mortal damage.
