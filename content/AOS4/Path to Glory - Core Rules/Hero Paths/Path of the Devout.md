*PRIEST HERO only.* Grants extra faith tricks on top of the Hero's normal prayers, culminating in battlefield-wide damage or healing rituals. See [[Hero Paths]] for the shared rank structure and [[How to Create a Hero]] for the wider campaign rules — remember to log each granted prayer by name on the army's Prayers Known column.

## Rank Tree

| Rank | Renown | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aspiring | 5–14 | Focused Meditation | Divine Blessing |
| Elite | 15–29 | Righteous Conviction (chanting 4) | Resurrection (chanting 7) |
| Mighty | 30–44 | Master of Ceremonies | Hallowed Champion |
| Legendary | 45+ | Ritual of Death (chanting 6) | Ritual of Life (chanting 8) |

## Aspiring (5–14 Renown)

**Focused Meditation** — Gain D3 ritual points during the deployment phase.

**Divine Blessing** — This PRIEST has WARD (6+).

## Elite (15–29 Renown)

**Righteous Conviction** *(chanting value 4)* — A friendly unit within 12" adds 1 to wound rolls for its attacks this turn.

**Resurrection** *(chanting value 7, once per battle)* — Return a slain friendly INFANTRY HERO to the battlefield within 3" of this PRIEST.

## Mighty (30–44 Renown)

**Master of Ceremonies** — When making a chanting roll for this PRIEST, roll 2 dice and pick 1.

**Hallowed Champion** — Friendly units wholly within 12" of this PRIEST have WARD (6+).

## Legendary (45+ Renown)

**Ritual of Death** *(chanting value 6)* — Each enemy unit suffers 1 mortal damage; if the chanting roll was 9+, D3 mortal damage instead.

**Ritual of Life** *(chanting value 8)* — Heal (1) each friendly unit; if the chanting roll was 9+, Heal (D3) instead.
