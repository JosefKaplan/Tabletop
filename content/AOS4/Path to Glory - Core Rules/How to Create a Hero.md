Core Path to Glory (AoS 4th edition) procedure for standing up a warband and its Warlord. This is system-generic — reusable across any campaign. For a specific campaign's starting points, army roster, and reward chain, see that campaign's own notes (e.g. its "Campaign Notes" page).

## 1. Pick a Faction and a Warlord

Choose your Faction, then pick one **Hero** from it to be your Warlord.

- Must be a single model (no unit-of-many-heroes).
- Must not be UNIQUE (no named characters as your starting Warlord).
- Must cost 300pts or less (Sons of Behemat get an exception).

Your Warlord is the anchor of the warband — track them separately from the rest of the roster since they get extra rules below.

## 2. Choose a Path

On recruitment your Warlord gains **5 Renown** and follows one of four progression Paths:

| Path | Who can take it | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| [[Path of the Warrior]] | Anyone | Combat upgrades, weapon mastery |
| [[Path of the Leader]] | Anyone | Command abilities, buffing the warband |
| [[Path of the Mage]] | Wizards only | Extra spells / arcane upgrades |
| [[Path of the Devout]] | Priests only | Extra prayers / faith upgrades |

Pick the Path that matches how you want the Warlord to grow — this can't be changed later, so match it to the faction's playstyle (e.g. a Grey Seer usually goes Mage, a Chaos Lord usually goes Warrior or Leader). See [[Hero Paths]] for the full 4-tier ability tree of each Path (or [[Hero Paths Canvas.canvas|the canvas]] for a visual branching view).

## 3. Build the Starting Order of Battle

Fill out the rest of the roster (Battleline, other Heroes, monsters, war machines, etc.) alongside the Warlord.

- Combined points of the whole army, including the Warlord, must be at or below your campaign's chosen starting size (check that campaign's notes for the exact cap — commonly 1,000pts for a small-scale campaign).
- Note each unit's starting size/points on your army's Order of Battle table so reinforcements later are easy to track.

## 4. Play a Battle

Every game you play generates two currencies:

- **Glory Points** — earned once per battle, based on the battle's points size. All that apply stack:

  | Battle size | Fought | +Major Victory | +Minor Victory | +General Survived |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1000–1250pts | 30 | +20 | +10 | +5 |
  | 1251–1750pts | 60 | +30 | +20 | +10 |
  | 1751–2250pts | 100 | +40 | +30 | +15 |
  | 2251pts+ | 150 | +50 | +40 | +20 |

  Source: [Wahapedia — AoS4 Path to Glory](https://wahapedia.ru/aos4/the-rules/ascension/).
- **Renown** — earned per unit, not per battle:
  - d3 Renown if the unit survived the battle.
  - 1 Renown if the unit was taken out of action.
  - Nominate one unit before the battle as **Favoured Warriors** — they earn an extra d6 Renown on top of the above. Your Warlord cannot be nominated.

Record both on the army's Glory & Renown Ledger after every game, before spending anything.

## 5. Spend Glory Points

Glory is the roster-building currency. Per aftermath sequence:

- Recruit **1** new unit into the Order of Battle — costs Glory equal to the unit's points value.
- Reinforce **1** existing unit — costs Glory equal to the points value of what's added.
- Retire any number of units you no longer want — free, but no Glory refund.

Spend it between battles, not mid-campaign-round — update the Order of Battle table immediately so points totals stay accurate for the next game.

## 6. Track Renown → Ranks

Renown accumulates **per unit** over the campaign and pushes it up through four rank tiers:

| Rank | Renown | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Aspiring | 5–14 | 1st Path ability (pick 1 of 2 options) |
| Elite | 15–29 | 2nd Path ability (pick 1 of 2 options) |
| Mighty | 30–44 | 3rd Path ability (pick 1 of 2 options) |
| Legendary | 45+ | 4th (final) Path ability — no further ranks |

Each rank-up grants a choice of 1-of-2 abilities from that unit's Path — Heroes pick from [[Hero Paths|Warrior/Leader/Mage(wizards)/Devout(priests)]]; regular units instead follow a Defender or Attacker Path. Log renown and current rank together on the Order of Battle table — don't just log the raw number and forget to check for a rank-up.

## 7. Log Everything Else As You Go

Anything that persists between battles belongs on the tracker, not just in memory:

- Artefacts and Blessings gained as campaign rewards.
- Spells known (Mage Path) or Prayers known (Devout Path) — these stack up as the Hero picks Path abilities that grant them, so list each one by name as it's gained, not just a count.
- Injuries/scars suffered by Heroes who were taken out of action.
- Triumphs or other one-off narrative bonuses.
- Which unit was Favoured Warriors that round (so you don't duplicate the bonus by accident).

## 8. Track Every Hero's Loadout

Heroes accumulate more than rank — track this on the army's Heroes table, not just the Order of Battle row:

- **Artefacts** — gained from campaign rewards or Glory spend; a Hero can only use as many as their army's rules allow, so note which one is actively equipped if they're holding a spare.
- **Spells known** — Mage Path Heroes add a new spell each time a Path ability grants one; list them by name.
- **Prayers known** — Devout Path Heroes add a new prayer the same way; list them by name.
- Warrior and Leader Path Heroes won't have entries in the Spells/Prayers columns — that's expected, leave them blank rather than filling with "N/A" clutter.

See your campaign's own army pages for the tracking tables.
